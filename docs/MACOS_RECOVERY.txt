diskOS macOS recovery
Keep the complete release download, logs and saved stock image.
UNKNOWN, timeout, app closed or USB lost after writing may have started:
Leave the Disc powered and connected for at least 25 minutes after the error.
Keep the computer awake and the lid open. Do not retry or disconnect during
this wait: the Disc may still be writing by itself. Then power-cycle it.
If it will not boot, power it off, hold Volume Down and connect USB for
mask-ROM mode (the screen stays black). Open diskOS Installer.app and use
Restore stock with the saved matching stock image. Restore the complete image;
never resume at a guessed block or bypass a refusal. Recovery is not guaranteed.
Keep the entire installer state folder, including stock generations and their
checksums. Run the app's Contents/MacOS/diskos-installer doctor to print its
state directory; DISKOS_INSTALLER_HOME overrides the normal per-user location.
If the backup is missing or damaged, preserve all state and use the original
verified FiiO ZIP for the Disc's existing firmware version in Restore stock.
Do not substitute a different version. If the version is unknown, get help.
For a blocked app, follow docs/INSTALL.md first-run Gatekeeper steps. For a
blocked bundled tool, move the app to a writable folder and reopen it; if still
blocked, download and extract a fresh complete release. Never replace individual
tools or libraries from unrelated downloads.
