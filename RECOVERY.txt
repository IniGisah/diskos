diskOS Windows recovery
Extract the whole ZIP before opening diskos-installer.exe. Keep _internal and
all sidecars together. Use diskos-installer-cli.exe for terminal commands.
UNKNOWN, timeout, app closed or USB lost after writing may have started:
Leave the Disc powered and connected for at least 25 minutes after the error.
Keep the computer awake. Do not retry or disconnect during this wait: the Disc
may still be writing by itself. Then power-cycle it. If it will not boot, power
it off, hold Volume Down and connect USB for mask-ROM (the screen stays black).
Use Restore stock, or the Linux installer with the same matching stock backup.
Restore the complete image; never resume at a guessed block or bypass a refusal.
Recovery is not guaranteed for every unit or failure.
Keep logs and the entire state folder: normally %LOCALAPPDATA%\diskos-installer,
or DISKOS_INSTALLER_HOME if set. Preserve stock\gNNNN\stock.bin, stock.sha256 and
stock.json, including uncommitted generations. stock\current.json selects the
committed backup; older installs may use stock\stock.bin and its checksum.
E141 (no saved stock image) or E145 (damaged backup record): preserve the state
folder and restore using the official FiiO firmware ZIP matching the Disc's
existing kernel and recovery firmware:
  diskos-installer-cli.exe restore-stock --firmware "C:\path\to\matching-firmware.zip"
The installer rebuilds stock and checks the Disc's firmware base before writing.
Do not substitute a different version or bypass a refusal. If you cannot
establish the matching version, keep the logs and get help.
Other errors (the UNKNOWN instructions above always take priority):
- App will not start, missing DLL, E102/E104/E105/E117/E123: check the download
  hash and extract the complete matching release into a new folder. Keep logs.
  For SmartScreen/Defender blocks, record the warning and get help; do not
  disable protection or download individual replacement DLLs.
- E101: use the flash-enabled release on native x64 Windows. A disabled build
  cannot be enabled with an environment variable. Use Linux if unsupported.
- E103: a bundled tool could not start. Use an executable filesystem, supply
  the required libraries, or rebuild the matching tools, then retry.
- E110: before writing starts, power off, hold Volume Down, connect a data USB
  cable directly to the PC. E111: unplug other Ingenic/mask-ROM devices.
- E112/E113/E114/E115: check the exact Disc's driver with Setup Windows driver,
  then repeat the USB check. Do not change drivers for unrelated devices.
- E118: administrator approval was cancelled; retry driver setup when ready.
  E119: ask an administrator to approve the helper with their credentials.
- E126: driver/device changed or setup failed; inspect the displayed Windows
  error, reconnect before writing and repeat setup/check. E127: unfamiliar
  driver; get help before replacing it. E128: driver installed but USB access
  failed; repeat the check with one Disc and keep the diagnostics if it fails.
- E116: keep the PC awake, check Windows power settings, then retry preflight.
- E120/E121/E122/E124/E125/E140/E143/E144: supply the matching official firmware
  ZIP and rebuild; do not pad/truncate an image or force an unknown base.
- E142: preserve state and logs; check folder access/free space and the displayed
  reason. Never edit a damaged backup or move the only copy to retry.
- E2xx: image build/firmware refused. Check free space and the official firmware
  download, keep the log and get help if it repeats; do not bypass validation.
- E301/E302/E303/E310/E321, UNKNOWN or an uncertain writer start: wait 25 minutes
  as above. E304: process could not start; keep the log and check the package.
- E320: the base gate refused before writing. Read the full reason and use the
  matching action below; E320 can report any of these F-code conditions.
- F202 (firmware mismatch): for install, apply FiiO's full update for the image's
  firmware base, let it finish and boot once, then retry. For restore, use stock
  firmware matching the Disc's existing kernel and recovery. If the version is
  unknown, preserve logs and get help. Never bypass the gate.
- F201/F203 (missing/damaged plan or image in memory differs from the plan):
  keep logs, verify the complete package and rebuild the image from matching
  official firmware. Start again from fresh mask-ROM; get help if it repeats.
  Never edit the plan, pad/truncate the image or bypass the gate.
- F204 (NAND not at power-on state): unplug the Disc, turn it fully off, then
  hold Volume Down and connect USB to enter fresh mask-ROM before retrying.
  E320 with this NAND-state reason requires the same power-off action.
- F205/F206/F207/F208 (base pages/markers unreadable or no good blocks),
  F209/F210/F211 (kernel/recovery missing or component exceeds its partition):
  preserve state and logs and get help. Changing the selected firmware does
  not repair these partitions. Never bypass the gate or force a write.
- E311/F1xx: writer failed or gave an unexpected result. Keep logs and restore
  the complete matching stock image; follow the wait above if completion is
  uncertain. For any other error, keep its full message and logs and get help.
